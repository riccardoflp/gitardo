# Who changed a line

- The line under the cursor says who last changed it, when, and the subject of that commit, at its end.
- The button with a person at the top right of an editor shows the blame of the whole file, with a bar in the color of the age of each line.
- `Alt+B` opens the commit of the line under the cursor in an editor tab, and `Shift+Alt+B` shows it in the graph.
- Hover the blame of a line to blame the file as it was before that commit, and walk back through the history of the line.
